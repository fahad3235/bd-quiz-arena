# BD Quiz Arena — Sources & verification notes

The question bank was curated with an accuracy-first policy: **only stable,
verifiable, non-disputed facts were included; anything shaky was dropped.**
Facts marked below were explicitly cross-checked on the web during curation
(2026-10-10). Everything else used is long-stable common knowledge.

## Verification policy
- Prefer historical milestones over current office-holders, live statistics,
  GDP/export dollar values, or recently-changed facts.
- Controversial topics (casualty figures, post-2024 political changes,
  disputed rankings) were excluded entirely.
- A smaller correct bank beats a bigger shaky one: 3 near-duplicate questions
  were removed and several candidate questions were dropped during curation.
